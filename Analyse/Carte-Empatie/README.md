# Carte d’Empathie
## 1️. The Persona

## Who is he?

- An expert fitness coach

- Wants to build a global brand

- Highly skilled in fitness

- But overwhelmed by admin work

> The problem is not his expertise — it’s organization and systems.

## 2️. Goal

## What does he want?

- Automate planning workflows

- Scale his client base efficiently

- Better monetize his expertise

> He wants a system that works without stress.

## 3️. What He Sees

## What’s happening around him?

- Competitors with sleek, professional websites

- Chaotic WhatsApp conversations

- Progress photos lost in the gallery

> He sees others looking more organized and professional.

## 4️. What He Says

## What does he express?

- “I need a unified client hub.”

- “I want speed and automation.”

- “I want professional prestige.”

> He wants structure and professionalism.

## 5️. What He Does

## What is he currently doing?

- Manually typing meal plans

- Copy-pasting routines

- Copy-pasting workouts

- Chasing clients for payments

> His workflow is repetitive and not scalable.

## 6️. What He Hears

## What influences him?

- Clients: “Where’s my plan?”

- Friends: “Build a website!”

- Market: “AI tools are coming…”

> There’s pressure from clients, friends, and industry trends.

## 7️. Think & Feel
### **Pains**

- Repetition fatigue

- Data fragmentation

- Weak digital authority

### **Gains**

- “I need a system.”

- Automated workflows

- Organized client progress
